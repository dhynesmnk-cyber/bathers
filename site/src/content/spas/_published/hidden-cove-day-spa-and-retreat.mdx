Hidden Cove sits on 7 acres of gum-treed land on the southern fringe of Hobart's peninsula, looking out over the Derwent River. It opened in September 2009 and has been run since by owner-operator Jerilee Lahey. That kind of setup tends to mean the person who designed the experience is also the one performing your treatment.

The focus is hands-on work. There are no pools, saunas, or plunge facilities here. What Hidden Cove offers is a treatment menu covering massage, reflexology, facials, body treatments, manicures and pedicures, and more. Reviews mention a spa with bubbles and river views from the massage room, though the full details of what's physically on site aren't spelled out anywhere publicly. Worth asking when you book if something specific matters to you.

At 4.7 stars across 146 Google reviews, the reception is consistently warm. Visitors particularly mention the quality of treatments and the care Lahey brings to the whole experience. Couples and groups feature prominently among those who've made the trip out.

East Tinderbox is about 20 minutes south of the city. At least one reviewer has confirmed the Uber route works fine, so a car isn't essential. The spa is open Monday and Wednesday through Sunday, closed Tuesdays, and runs by appointment only. Saturday hours stretch to 8pm, which makes it the most flexible day if you're after an afternoon treatment. Group bookings and hens days are specifically catered for. Call 0407 478 269 to arrange a time.
