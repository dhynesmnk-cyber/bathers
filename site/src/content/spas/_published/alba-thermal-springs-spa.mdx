On a peninsula where the land tips toward two bays, geothermal springs surface at 282 Browns Rd in Fingal. Alba Thermal Springs & Spa has been built around this source, with a contemporary design set within native bushland.

The pool circuit spans indoor, open-air, and outdoor thermal options, with a rain pool and herbal-infused botanical pools rounding out the water offering. <Pull>The springs draw on geothermal water, though the specific mineral composition isn't documented, and temperatures aren't published — if that detail matters for your planning, call (03) 5985 0900.</Pull>

The treatment side covers massage, body treatments, and water and steam therapy as core offerings. The facial menu draws on two skincare ranges — Sodashi and Vanessa Megan — giving you choice in approach. Half-day rituals and full-day packages are available if you want a structured day rather than drop-in sessions.

Food is handled by Thyme, which runs across five different formats depending on when you arrive: Brunch Thyme, Dinner Thyme, Tea Thyme, Pool House Picnic, and Picnic Thyme. The kitchen sources locally and seasonally.

<Pull>Accommodation sits in the dunes above the springs — a practical option if you're coming from Melbourne and want to stay the night.</Pull> Gift packages run from The Drift and The Coast to Champagne & Caviar and The Jewel. A Springs Club membership is available for regulars, though what it covers specifically isn't detailed publicly.

Alba is open daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, closed only on Christmas Day. The long hours mean an early-morning session before the day fills up is genuinely possible.
