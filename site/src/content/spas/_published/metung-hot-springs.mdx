The water at Metung Hot Springs comes from 500 metres below East Gippsland, rising at 45°C and carrying the minerals it's picked up on the way. That's the whole premise of the place, and the site is arranged to make the most of it across three bathing precincts: Bathing Ridge, Lagoon Precinct, and Hilltop Escarpment, each on naturally undulating ground with views over the Gippsland Lakes.

Day visitors have access to all three precincts, plus saunas on site. The safari-style glamping tents, which come with private soaking barrels on the deck, are reserved for overnight guests aged 18 and older.

On weekends, a day spa opens up alongside the bathing. The treatment menu runs to relaxation massages, facials, Synthesis Organics native botanical treatments, and Larn'wa Aboriginal Lore wellness rituals. All of it requires a bathing package, and all of it is Saturdays and Sundays only. In peak periods, bookings fill 4 to 6 weeks out, so this is one to sort before you leave home rather than once you've arrived in East Gippsland.

The place has 746 Google reviews sitting at 4.4 stars, with repeat mentions of the lake views, the barrel baths, and how well the site is maintained. Early morning, before day visitors arrive, gets a particular nod for the quiet.

A café handles refreshments between soaks. Towels, robes, and lockers are all available to hire. One thing worth knowing before you pack: hair dryers aren't provided in the change rooms.

The site is open every day of the year from 9am to 6pm, with guests out of the pools 15 minutes before closing. Multi-visit passes come in 2-visit and 5-visit options if you're planning to make a stretch of it. A 10% surcharge applies on public holidays.
