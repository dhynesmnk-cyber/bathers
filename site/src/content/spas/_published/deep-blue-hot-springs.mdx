At 1 Worm Bay Road in Warrnambool, the Deep Blue sits right where the land runs out. The coast is the backdrop; the pools are fed by geothermal mineral water drawn from deep underground. It works as a hotel stay, a day trip, or something in between.

The Hot Springs Sanctuary is the main draw: open-air and indoor pools, cascading thermal waterfalls, and water that's mineral-rich by description, though the specific composition isn't published. If a particular mineral is what you're after, it's worth a call before you book. What's not in question is the setting. Outdoor pools with an unobstructed view of the Southern Ocean is a reasonable argument for making the drive to Warrnambool.

<Pull>Outdoor pools, geothermal water drawn from deep underground, and the Southern Ocean right there as backdrop.</Pull>

Inside, the Sanctuary runs to sensory caves and a salt therapy room. The salt therapy session goes for 45 minutes and pairs guided meditation with salt air exposure. It's the quieter option, worth building into a longer visit rather than treating as an afterthought.

Sessions are structured and timed across the day. The twilight slot (7:30pm to 9:30pm) is adults and older teens only, which tends to make it the calmer end of the day. Entry includes robe, towel, and locker hire, so your bag can stay light. Non-slip footwear is required, glassware is out, and a shower before you get in is compulsory.

<Pull>Robe, towel, and locker hire are all included, the list of things to pack is genuinely short.</Pull>

Children under 5 can't use the Sanctuary. Kids aged 5 to 15 are welcome at day sessions, with an adult within arm's reach throughout.

Day access starts at $55 per adult through the Breakfast & Bathe package, or $68 per adult for Soak & Graze, which adds a food component and a 2:30pm to 4:30pm soaking window. An evening tasting and hot springs stay begins at $645, and a twilight ritual with a chef's tasting menu is available as an $85 per person addition. Overnight packages, which include the hotel and indoor pool access, start at $449 Sunday to Thursday and step up from there on weekends. The hotel holds a 4.3 rating across more than 1,600 Google reviews.

Book ahead, particularly for twilight sessions and weekends. Those slots fill up, and the timed structure means there's no casual walk-in flexibility once a session is full.
