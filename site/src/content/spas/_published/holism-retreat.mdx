Holism Retreat is a treatment-focused day spa inside the M Gallery Hotel Chadstone on Dandenong Road. There are no pools or heat rooms here. The offer is massage, skincare, facials, and LED light therapy, done well in a quiet setting.

Phone use isn't permitted before or during treatments, and organic herbal teas are available in the lounge while you wait or wind down. Lockers are on hand for valuables. The whole setup is geared toward the appointment itself rather than anything surrounding it.

<Pull>With 352 Google reviews averaging 4.8 stars, the consistent thread is how the therapists handle pressure adjustment and problem-area work, remedial massage in particular draws repeat visitors.</Pull>

A few things worth knowing before you book. Facial treatments come with contraindications around cosmetic injectables, prescription retinols, and active infections. Worth flagging anything relevant when you book rather than on the day.

Full payment is taken at the time of booking, a 10% surcharge applies on weekends, and cancellations within 24 hours attract a 100% fee. Treat the booking as confirmed once it's made. Gift vouchers are valid for three years from purchase, which gives them some genuine flexibility as a gift.

<Pull>Arrive 15 minutes early. The spa asks this of all clients, and it's the kind of place where the time is actually useful rather than just administrative.</Pull>

Hours run Monday to Thursday 10am to 6pm, Friday until 7pm, Saturday 9am to 6pm, and Sunday 9am to 5pm. You don't need to be staying at M Gallery to get in. Visitors booking treatments directly are welcome alongside hotel guests.
