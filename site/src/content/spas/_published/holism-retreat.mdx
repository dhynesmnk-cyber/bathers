Holism Retreat sits inside the MGallery Hotel Chadstone on Dandenong Road in Malvern East — a treatment spa built around hands-on work: remedial and relaxation massage, holistic skincare, facial treatments, and LED light therapy. The setup is deliberately low-key: no phone use before or during treatments, complimentary organic herbal teas in the lounge, lockers for valuables, and a request that clients arrive 15 minutes early.

<Pull>Holism Retreat is a treatment spa inside the MGallery Hotel Chadstone, built around hands-on work rather than a circuit of facilities.</Pull>

The treatment list is focused. Massage is the main draw — remedial for specific issues, relaxation for general tension — with the option to add LED light therapy. Facials come with a few contraindications worth noting before you book: if you've had cosmetic injectables in the last two weeks, or you're on prescription retinols or vitamin A products, or you're dealing with an active infection or cold sore, facials aren't available at that visit. It's a reasonable precaution, but worth factoring in when you plan.

Pricing isn't listed publicly, though a 10% surcharge applies on weekends — Saturday and Sunday both. Full payment is required at booking, and the cancellation policy is firm: within 24 hours of your appointment, the full cost is charged. No-shows are treated the same way. If you cancel with more notice, a credit is held against your account for 12 months. Gift vouchers run for three years from purchase.

<Pull>With 352 Google reviews averaging 4.8 stars, massage therapists come up repeatedly by name — a sign a place keeps its staff.</Pull>

Hours run Monday to Thursday 10am–6pm, Friday until 7pm, Saturday 9am–6pm, and Sunday 9am–5pm. You don't need to be staying at the MGallery to book — just call (03) 9108 3388 or use the website. Book ahead; later-in-week slots fill quickly.
