Subway Sauna sits in Vault 13 beneath Flinders Street, a long-running gay men's sauna occupying one of the CBD's more atmospheric addresses — a brick vault under one of Melbourne's most recognisable train stations. The name isn't metaphorical.

The venue runs across 30 serviced rooms, two cruise areas kept separate from each other, two private rooms, video lounges, and a cafe and bar that stays open round the clock, which matters given the place never closes. Whether you turn up at noon on a Wednesday or 3am on a Saturday, the doors are open.

The wet area has been recently renovated, though staged works mean not every part of the venue finishes simultaneously. The specific sauna type — whether traditional Finnish sauna, steam room, or both — isn't confirmed in the available record, so worth confirming directly before you go if that's the main draw.
