On Glenferrie Road in Hawthorn, ikigai runs as a Japanese-inspired treatment studio. There's no thermal circuit here, no pools or saunas. The whole offer is hands-on work: scalp, facial, and body treatments, with the Head Spa and Scalp Detox as the thing most people come for.

That signature treatment follows a fixed sequence drawn from traditional Japanese head spa practice. Hot stones open the session, a shoulder massage runs through the middle of it, cooling stones close things out, and a blow-dry finishes it off. It's a considered structure rather than something improvised on the day. For facial work, the studio uses Esthermax Hydro Jelly Masks; the body treatment draws on vegan, wild-harvested botanicals sourced from the Blue Mountains.
