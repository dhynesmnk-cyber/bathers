On Glenferrie Road in Hawthorn, ikigai Head Spa and Wellness runs a treatment-focused operation built around Japanese head spa rituals. There are no pools or saunas here — this is a hands-on therapy venue, and the scalp is the thing.

The practice draws on traditional Japanese healing, with treatments that include deep scalp work, facial detox, and body therapy, all using natural botanicals. The space is set up in themed rooms, which reviewers consistently describe as cosy and carefully considered. <Pull>A 5-star rating across 96 Google reviews suggests the execution matches the concept.</Pull> Reviewer accounts mention hot stones at the start of sessions, cooling stones at the end, shoulder massage woven into scalp treatments, and hair blow-dried before you leave — the kind of sequencing that suggests someone has thought carefully about what a treatment should feel like start to finish.
