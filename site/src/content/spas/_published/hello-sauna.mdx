Hello Sauna is a wood-fired sauna that sets up on the foreshore at Golightly Park, Point Lonsdale, close enough to the water that the cold plunge is the ocean itself. The setup is mobile and seasonal, and it's built around the idea that a sauna next to the sea is its own complete experience.

The sauna runs on wood fire, positioned outdoors by the beach. Sessions are short and structured: 30 minutes or an hour, available for solo visitors or groups. <Pull>The cold option here is the ocean, a short walk from the sauna door, which makes the hot-cold contrast easy to repeat.</Pull> The venue's origins sit in cold-water swimming culture, and reviews consistently mention the cycle of sauna, swim, sauna as the intended rhythm.

The venue rates 5 stars across 67 Google reviews. Recurring themes in reviews are the welcome from the host and how well the foreshore location works in practice, with the water close enough to make the hot-cold cycle repeat easily.

Current hours are Monday 4–6pm, Friday 4–8pm, and Saturday and Sunday 7am–7pm. <Pull>The venue has run as a seasonal pop-up in previous years (August through November), so if you're planning a visit outside that window, confirm the schedule is still running.</Pull> The address is the Caravan and Camping area of Golightly Park, Bowen Road, though as a mobile setup the exact positioning can shift.

On pricing, Hello Sauna lists two sets of rates (30 minutes and one hour each appear at two different price points) and the site doesn't explain which applies to which booking type. Call 0421 356 415 to confirm what you'll pay before you book.

Solo and group sessions are both available.
