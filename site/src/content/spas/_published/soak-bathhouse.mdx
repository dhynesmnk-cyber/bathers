Ground floor of a River Street building in South Yarra, Soak operates as a communal mineral bathhouse that opens earlier than almost anywhere else in Melbourne — 6:30am daily — and runs until midnight on weekends to catch the post-dinner crowd. It's a modern setup, not a heritage conversion: the design is clean, the circuit is straightforward, and the pricing tiers let you choose how much comes with your session.

The water circuit centres on magnesium-rich mineral pools, with hot spas both indoors and outside in the open air, plus a cold plunge for contrast work. Heat options in the communal area include a dry cedarwood sauna and a steam room. Infrared sauna sits in the private treatment category rather than the main circuit, so if that's something you want, factor it into your booking rather than assuming it's included.

<Pull>The open-air hot spas make the most obvious case for arriving after dark on a Friday — the late midnight closing on weekends isn't accidental.</Pull>

Entry pricing has a few layers worth understanding. The Early Bird rate — $39 weekdays, $49 Friday to Sunday — covers a 60-minute session with complimentary tea, water, and towel hire already in the package, which is good value if you're an early riser. The standard Soak starts at $59 weekdays. The Soak Essentials tier (from $89 weekdays, $99 weekends) runs 90 minutes and adds two boutique beverages, a wellness snack, a complimentary robe, and padlock hire — closer to a longer ritual than a quick dip.

Treatments — a 50-minute relaxation massage, infrared sauna, or LED facial — can be added to any session or booked as standalone appointments. Pairing a 50-minute massage with a 90-minute soak starts at $169 weekdays and $198 on weekends.

<Pull>Towel hire is included with the Early Bird, which removes one of the more common reasons to arrive underprepared.</Pull>

South Yarra is one of four Australian locations; the others are in Brisbane, Gold Coast, and Sydney. This entry covers River Street only — hours, pricing, and specifics will differ at other sites. Book ahead, particularly for weekend evenings and Essentials packages, and note that early morning slots give you the full facility with fewer people competing for the outdoor spas.
