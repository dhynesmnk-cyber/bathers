P3 Recovery on Bay Street in Port Melbourne is built around one idea: getting your body back to functional as efficiently as possible. Ice baths, saunas, contrast therapy, compression systems, and myotherapy are the core offering — no pools for leisurely soaking, no treatment menus padded with extras. It's a purposeful circuit for people who've put their body through something and want to do something about it.

The ice baths are described consistently as well-maintained and part of a clear contrast therapy flow — moving between heat and cold in a sequence rather than using either in isolation. Compression systems add a passive, circulatory layer on top, and the on-site myotherapy means you can address specific muscle or soft tissue issues in the same visit rather than booking elsewhere.

<Pull>The staff know members by name and run check-ins while you're there — a small thing that makes a real difference in a venue where you're regularly pushing yourself.</Pull>

The facility sits at 292 Bay Street, in easy reach of the Port Melbourne and South Melbourne fitness crowd, though members come from further out. It opens at 6am on weekdays — early enough to fit a session before work — and runs through to 9pm, with shorter weekend hours (8am–4pm Saturday and Sunday).

The Google Places record shows 205 reviews at a 5-star average. What comes through in the written feedback isn't just satisfaction with the equipment — it's that the team is genuinely attentive, the space is consistently clean and well set up, and the contrast therapy and compression in combination produce a noticeable difference in recovery. Members range from athletes logging high training weeks to people who simply want to feel better after a demanding stretch.
