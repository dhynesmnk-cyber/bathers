On a hillside in Fingal, at the southern tip of the Mornington Peninsula, Peninsula Hot Springs is a sprawling geothermal complex that's hard to pin down. It's far bigger than a bathhouse and more substantive than a day spa — with over 50 bathing experiences in the standard package and more than 70 site-wide, set across native bushland and rolling peninsula hills.

The geothermal mineral water feeds pools at various levels of ambition. The Hilltop Pool offers 360-degree views across the peninsula; the Cave Pool draws on Japanese Alps bathing traditions; the Moon View and Lakeside pools offer their own orientations; and the Aqua Therapy Pool adds shiatsu jets and stretching elements for something closer to active hydrotherapy. The Moonah Pool rounds out the main circuit. Additional geothermal pools scatter through the Bathing Gully precinct, which also contains an underground sauna.

Heat options extend well beyond the pools. Wet and dry saunas and a Turkish Hamam with a traditional Kese Mitt scrub using copper bowls are on offer. On the cold side, multiple plunge pools are set up explicitly for contrast therapy. Treatments run from 45 to 75 minutes and cover massage, facials, and body work. There's a Reflexology Walk with ten stone surfaces for acupressure and Nepalese-inspired mineral massaging showers. Pregnancy treatments are available too.

The broader site includes an amphitheatre with geothermal bathing and capacity for live performances, a Moroccan Pavilion with day beds, a Sound Dome built for deep rest, and a Sun Lounge with deck chairs and open views. Overnight accommodation is available, with private in-room hot spring baths, access to outdoor geothermal pools, and breakfast buffet included.

The Bath House (Revitalise package) is open to all ages. The Spa Dreaming Centre operates adults-only, with entry restricted to guests 16 and over. Pricing moves with the day and time — weekday earlybird slots sit at the lower end of the range, Friday and Saturday evenings push toward the higher end. Multi-visit passes and monthly memberships work for regulars, and group packages scale up to parties of 30 or more.

Monday through Thursday and Sunday, the site opens at 7am and closes at 11pm. Friday and Saturday run longer — doors open at 5am and close at 2am. Book ahead, particularly for weekends and evening slots, which fill quickly.
