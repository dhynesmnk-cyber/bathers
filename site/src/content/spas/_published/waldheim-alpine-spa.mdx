The Waldheim Alpine Spa sits inside Cradle Mountain Lodge on the edge of the Tasmanian Wilderness World Heritage Area, beside the Pencil Pine River and surrounded by ancient forest. It's a treatment spa first, facials, body rituals, a considered menu of native-ingredient treatments, but its centrepiece is the Sanctuary: a private circuit of hot tub, cool plunge pool, sauna and steam room enclosed by King Billy pines.

<Pull>The Sanctuary is a private circuit of hot tub, cool plunge pool, sauna and steam room enclosed by King Billy pines, one of the more unusual settings for a hot-cold session in Australia.</Pull>

The treatment list leans into Australian native botanicals. There's a foot bathing ceremony using melaleuca flower and strawberry gum, a full-body ritual built around lime caviar and Kakadu plum, and a range of facials, body wraps and signature rituals. All treatments use the Waterlily artisanal spa collection. It's a well-considered menu for a remote mountain location.

The spa hasn't published pricing publicly, so ring them on (03) 6492 2133 before you plan around a particular budget. Hours are 9am to 5pm daily.

The spa rates 4.4 out of 5 across 82 Google reviews, and individual staff members are named and praised with unusual specificity in what people mention, a sign of a small team doing the personal side of things well.

You don't need to be a lodge guest to book, day visitors are taken. That said, advance bookings are strongly recommended. Cradle Mountain Road is a long drive from anywhere, and arriving without a confirmed booking risks a wasted trip. The Sanctuary in particular, being private, is likely to fill on busier days. Book before you make the drive.
