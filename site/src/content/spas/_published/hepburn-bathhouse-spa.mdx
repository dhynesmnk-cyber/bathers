The building at 1 Mineral Springs Reserve Road has been in the bathhouse business since 1895, making it one of the older continuously operating bathing venues in the country. The springs beneath Hepburn Springs produce mineral-rich water that feeds the pools directly. The water comes from a natural source rather than being treated and reconstituted.

The venue divides into distinct zones. The main mineral bathing area is open to all ages; the Sanctuary Mineral Bathing area is adults-only (16 and above) and is where the more structured circuit lives: heated mineral pools, a salt pool, a dry sauna, and a Contrast Therapy ritual built around heat, cold mineral immersion, and a cold-water cascade from an overhead bucket. That cascade is the cold element documented here — it's not a freestanding plunge pool.

The Sanctuary has attracted consistent praise from visitors who made the upgrade, with reviewers describing it as noticeably quieter and more manageable than the main area. The overall rating sits at 3.5 across more than 1,500 reviews. The gap in experience between the two tiers is significant — if you're choosing, that context is useful.

Pricing is tiered accordingly, and peak rates apply on public holidays, school holidays, and other busy periods. Prices can change without notice, so confirm current rates before you go. Hours run 9am to 7pm Sunday through Thursday, and extend to 9pm on Friday and Saturday. The venue is closed on Christmas Day and Good Friday.

The Hepburn Pavilion café is on site and runs its own hours: 8:30am to 4pm on weekdays and Sundays, and to 5pm on Saturdays. Accommodation is also offered through the venue, which makes this a plausible base if you're spending more than a day in the Daylesford-Hepburn area. If you're coming on a weekend without a booking, aim for the morning — Friday afternoons in particular pick up in the later hours.
