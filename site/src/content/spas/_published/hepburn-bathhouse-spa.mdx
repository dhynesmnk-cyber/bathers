The Hepburn Bathhouse has been drawing people to the western slopes of the Great Dividing Range since 1895, when the mineral springs at Hepburn Springs were already well regarded enough to warrant a proper public facility. That makes it one of the oldest continuously operating bathhouses in the country. The springs themselves are the foundation of the whole thing: natural mineral water, not piped-in or manufactured, as they have been from the start.

The two main bathing areas work quite differently from each other. The Salt Pool is general admission and draws on the same natural springs. The Sanctuary Mineral Bathing area is the premium option, quieter and limited to ages 16 and over. The reviews bear this out: visitors who've done both consistently say the Sanctuary is worth the difference, especially if you've come a distance and want more than a busy public pool.

For heat and cold: a dry sauna handles the heat side, and the cold comes via an overhead mineral-water cascade rather than a conventional plunge pool. The venue also runs a Contrast Therapy ritual, a structured sequence of heat, cold, and mineral immersion that's sold as a named experience. The water is mineral-rich throughout, though the specific mineral composition isn't something the venue has published.

The bathhouse sits in the Mineral Springs Reserve, close to Daylesford and about 90 minutes from Melbourne. Accommodation is available on-site, as are food and functions. The on-site café runs Monday to Friday 8:30am to 4pm, Saturday to 5pm, and Sunday to 4pm.

With 1,538 Google reviews averaging 3.5 stars, opinion is mixed, and the pattern is consistent: reviews for the Sanctuary skew warmer than those for general admission. The recurring advice from satisfied visitors is to book the premium option and time it for a weekday if you can manage it.

Opening hours are Monday to Thursday and Sunday 9am to 7pm, Friday and Saturday to 9pm. The venue closes on Christmas Day and Good Friday. Pricing isn't fixed year-round: peak rates apply on public holidays, school holidays, and other busy periods, so it's worth checking before you book.
