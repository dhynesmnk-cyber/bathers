On Rose Street in Essendon, Sóma Bathhouse keeps things focused: a magnesium hot spa, an infrared sauna, and a cold plunge. That's the whole circuit, and the point is doing it properly rather than offering more of it. A 4.8 rating from over a hundred reviews, with cleanliness and design mentioned consistently, suggests the approach is working.

The core offering is the Sóma Trio, a 60-minute session built around temperature contrast. The infrared sauna runs at 60 to 70 degrees and uses both near and far infrared wavelengths, which gives it a different feel from a conventional dry sauna. After that, the ice bath. After that, the magnesium hot spa at 39 degrees. The order matters; each stage makes the next one count.

The venue is small, which works in its favour. You can book communal or private sessions, so if sharing the circuit doesn't appeal, say so when you reserve. For something in between, the Zen Zone sessions (no talking permitted) run on Tuesday mornings, Thursday afternoons, and Sunday afternoons within the regular schedule.

A few reviewers mention arriving on a weekday afternoon to find the place largely to themselves, though that's not guaranteed. Weekdays run two sessions with a midday break, weekends are continuous, and Monday is closed. For pricing, the booking flow on the website is the clearest place to look. First-timers get an introductory offer valid for three weeks, but the window opens on purchase rather than on your first visit, so buy it when you're ready to go. The phone number is 0423 238 408.
