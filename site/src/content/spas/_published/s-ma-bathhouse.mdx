Sóma Bathhouse occupies a small venue on Rose Street in Essendon, built around a three-part circuit: infrared sauna, ice bath, and magnesium hot spa. The format is deliberate — temperature contrast therapy, working heat and cold against each other. Both communal and private bathing experiences are available, so you can choose how social you want the session to be.

The magnesium hot spa is the centrepiece. The circuit itself is well-defined and straightforward. What's clear from the venue's setup is that this isn't a sprawling facility — reviewers describe it as small but spacious, spotlessly clean, with soft lighting and thoughtful design.

Sóma runs quiet sessions on Tuesday 9:00am–1:30pm, Thursday 3:00–8:00pm, and Sunday 2:00–6:00pm. The venue is open Tuesday through Friday in two blocks (9:00am–1:30pm and 3:00–8:00pm), and Saturday and Sunday from 9:30am–6:00pm. Mondays are closed.

Book ahead for the quiet sessions and weekend slots — a venue this size fills quickly when a specific time matters. Phone 0423 238 408 or book through somabathhouse.com.
