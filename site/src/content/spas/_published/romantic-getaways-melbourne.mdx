On five acres of mountain property at Narbethong, Woodlands Rainforest Retreat is built around the idea that bathing should be entirely yours. Every pool and tub is reserved for one couple at a time — there's no shared circuit, no timetabled sittings alongside strangers.

The bathing options span a few distinct styles. Outdoors, there are rock pools and cedar hot tubs fed by natural mineral water drawn from 80 metres underground, set among walled gardens, lawns, and secluded garden areas. Inside, the Roman bathhouse goes in a different direction entirely: a domed ceiling, marble, and mosaics — more Mediterranean than mountain, which is either a feature or a warning depending on your taste. The mineral water is heated throughout.

The grounds include Japanese-style gardens, a lake, and landscaped tropical plantings. Accommodation is in Japanese-style villas, and the restaurant and bar overlooks the forest. A Japanese Charcoal BBQ lunch is also on the menu. The whole property is adults-only.

Treatment options include couples massages at 50 or 80 minutes, and body treatments at the Spa De Marrakech. The couples date package is $615 per person — it's not fully documented which elements are bundled versus add-ons, so confirm the inclusions when you book.

With 96 Google reviews averaging 4.3 stars, the retreat draws consistent praise for privacy, the surrounding nature, and staff flexibility. A handful of guests have noted that the property's exterior shows its age, which is what happens when you build in an actual rainforest.

Opening hours run 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM daily. Given the private-bathing model, booking ahead is essential — there are a finite number of couples the property can host at any one time. Narbethong is about 90 minutes northeast of Melbourne via the Maroondah Highway, close to Lake Eildon and the Warburton area.
