Peninsula Sauna & Spa is a suburban men's sauna on Cumberland Drive in Seaford, roughly a kilometre from the bay. The venue's own web presence is light on facility details — the sauna type (traditional, steam, or otherwise) isn't confirmed in the available record — but the programming tells you something about the place: it runs a structured calendar of themed sessions rather than just open-door hours.

<Pull>Three recurring sessions anchor the week — Xposed Nude Sunday, Mates Rates, and HUMP Nude Night — each with its own timeslot and, presumably, its own crowd.</Pull>

Sunday is the accessible entry point: Xposed Nude Sunday runs noon to 8pm at $25. The weeknight sessions — Mates Rates and HUMP Nude Night, both 6pm to 10:30pm — extend the programming into the evening on days when general hours close at 6:30pm. Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday stay open until 10:30pm; Tuesday, Friday, and Saturday close at 6:30pm; Sunday wraps at 8pm.
