Down in the lower ground of a Little Collins Street hotel, V Hotel Spa runs a treatment-heavy operation with a steam room, private spa baths, and one of the longer massage menus you'll find in the CBD. It's not a circuit bathhouse — there's no pool to move between, no cold plunge, no sauna in the traditional sense — but if what you're after is a couple of hours on a table with skilled hands working through a specific problem, this is where reviewers end up.

The heat offering centres on a steam room and a hydro-steam facility, with spa baths available as part of treatments rather than as standalone drop-in facilities. <Pull>Hot stone massage uses water-heated stones, which is the detail worth knowing if you're choosing between that and deep tissue on a cold afternoon.</Pull> The venue uses premium natural products free of chemicals.

The massage menu is broad: deep tissue, hot stone, acupressure, aromatherapy, foot reflexology, remedial (claimable on private health funds with registered therapists), pregnancy massages, and couples massages. Body treatments run to scrubs and hydration masks alongside facials. Package bookings that combine foot spa, massage, and facial across two or more hours seem to be what most visitors go for, based on the review record.

With a 4.8 rating across 824 Google reviews, V Hotel Spa sits at the top end of the local field — reviewers consistently single out therapist attentiveness and the quality of individual treatment over the absence of communal facilities. <Pull>More than one person has noted that it holds its own against the larger branded spas in the city, specifically on the quality of what happens in the treatment room.</Pull> Complimentary tea and scented towels come up repeatedly as touches that lift the experience.

Open daily from 10:00am to 9:00pm, with a surcharge on weekends and public holidays. Reach them on (03) 9629 6600 or relax@vhotelspa.com.au. Gift vouchers are available.
