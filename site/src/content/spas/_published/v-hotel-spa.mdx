Below street level on Little Collins Street, tucked beneath the Savoy Hotel, V Hotel Spa runs a full treatment menu out of a space that reviewers consistently describe as beautifully designed and noticeably calm. The water elements here (private jacuzzi, foot spa, hydro-steam) sit inside packages rather than operating as standalone offerings. There's no communal soaking. What's on offer is a long, well-executed treatment session with therapists who adjust pressure and focus on request.

The signature packages run 2.5 to 3 hours and combine things like a foot spa, deep tissue or acupressure massage, and a facial, with some including a private jacuzzi or hydro-steam component depending on what you book. Facials use Perfect Potion organic products. Remedial massage therapists are registered with health funds, which is worth knowing if you're planning to claim. The broader menu covers hot rock massage, pregnancy massage, foot reflexology, aromatherapy, body scrubs, masks, and exfoliation treatments. It's a long list.

Several reviewers note that therapists checked in during treatments and adjusted focus on request. That kind of attentiveness is what separates a genuinely good session from one that's merely long.

<Pull>With a rating of 4.8 from over 825 Google reviews, the feedback is consistently strong.</Pull>

Reviewers put it up against Melbourne's bigger-name spas and find it holds up. A few recurring details, complimentary tea and snacks on arrival, scented towels, suggest the venue has thought carefully about what surrounds the treatments, not only the treatments themselves. One reviewer flagged no pool and no view as a minor caveat, then called it the best spa experience they'd had in Melbourne. That probably tells you where the priorities lie.

The lower-ground location and a treatment menu stretching to three hours means it's genuinely possible to lose most of an afternoon down there, which for some people is exactly the point.

Hours are daily, 10:00 am to 9:00 pm. A surcharge applies on weekends and public holidays, so it's worth factoring in if you're booking a Saturday package. Couple massages and hens' day packages are available for group bookings. Gift vouchers are offered. Reach them on (03) 9629 6600 or at relax@vhotelspa.com.au to check package availability and book.
