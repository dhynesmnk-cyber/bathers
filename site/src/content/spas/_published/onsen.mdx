On Henley Beach Road in Torrensville, Onsen runs a tight, purpose-built recovery circuit that splits its time between heat and cold. The traditional sauna sits at 90 to 95°C. The contrast therapy pairs a warm mineral bath with a cold mineral plunge, repeated across ten rounds in a 25-minute block. Everything here is structured and timed, which means sessions move quickly and the place stays orderly.

<Pull>The traditional sauna sits at 90 to 95°C, and the contrast therapy runs ten cycles of warm mineral bath against cold mineral plunge in 25 minutes flat.</Pull>

The format suits people who want a defined protocol rather than an open-ended soak. A single contrast bath session is $44 for 25 minutes. The sauna options, both traditional and private infrared, come in at $27 for 25 minutes. If you're planning to come regularly, the 10-pack at $190 gets the per-session cost down considerably, and the weekly memberships go further still: three sessions a week for $49, five for $59, or daily access for $69, all valid for 12 months.

Google reviews from the venue's first months run consistently high (5 stars from 57 ratings). The praise centres on two things: the place is kept very clean, and the owners Josh and Jimmy walk new visitors through each area before they start. Compression boots are wiped between uses.

<Pull>Reviews point to a venue run with care. Compression boots are cleaned between uses, sessions are explained to newcomers, and the layout stands out among similar places in Adelaide.</Pull>

Sessions can be taken solo or with a companion, and private experiences are available. Compression therapy ($27 for 25 minutes) is offered alongside the bathing and sauna options for anyone building a fuller recovery session.

Weekday hours run in two blocks: 7:00 to 9:30am and 3:30 to 9:00pm, which fits around a working day. Weekends run straight through from 8:00am to 6:00pm. Because sessions are structured, slots fill quickly. Book ahead before you make the trip.
