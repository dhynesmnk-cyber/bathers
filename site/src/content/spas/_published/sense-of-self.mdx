Behind a rusted door off Easey Street, a two-storey brick warehouse holds Sense of Self, known to regulars as SOS. The draw is a bathing circuit built around a magnesium-rich pool, with a steam room, cold plunge, and sauna rounding it out. Massage treatments and a hammam ritual are available on the same visit if you want more than the circuit.

The magnesium pool is the warm centre of things. Reviewers describe the temperature as genuinely comfortable, though the pool runs small relative to how many people a sitting can hold, so a busy timeslot will feel it. The cold plunge is compact, barely a one-person fit at a time, and cold enough to earn its name. The steam room is what regulars tend to single out as the standout: larger than the other facilities and seriously hot. The sauna fits around five or six comfortably.

<Pull>At 4.7 stars from over 1,600 Google reviews, the consensus is strong. Most of the praise goes toward the hammam and the massage offering, with the circuit's compact size the most common caveat.</Pull>

Between rounds, there's a dedicated space to sit, have tea, and let your heart rate settle before going again. Staff keep the place moving, wiping down wet floors and offering drinks to guests. Private showers come stocked with soap and shampoo.

The hammam ritual pairs steam with a scrub and is the treatment most often mentioned in reviews. If you'd rather do it yourself, the Scrub Station offers a Hammam Kit: a reusable bag with a kessa glove and two empty vessels for products.

Tuesday through Sunday the doors open at 6:30am and run through to 10:45pm. Monday starts at 10am. The bathing areas are phone-free, so leave it in the locker. Popular timeslots fill quickly, so booking ahead is worth doing. Vouchers come in digital or physical form and cover bathing, treatments, or products from the retail space, which makes them a practical gift if you're looking for one.
