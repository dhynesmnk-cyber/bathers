City Cave Braybrook sits inside the Home and Co. retail complex on Ballarat Road. What it offers is more focused: float therapy, infrared sauna, massage, and red light therapy add-on, all under one roof on the western fringe of Melbourne.

The float pool is filled with Epsom salt water and held at body temperature, so you lose track of where you end up and the water begins. The infrared sauna works differently from a traditional steam or rock sauna: radiant heat that moves through the air without ultraviolet rays, which some people find easier to sit in for longer. There's no cold plunge here, so if you're after a contrast circuit, this isn't the place for it.

What you do get is a clean, treatment-led menu you can mix and match. The Flauna package ($129) pairs the sauna with a float. The Complete Wellness package ($229) adds a 45-minute massage on top. Massage options run from remedial and sports to pregnancy and relaxation, and sessions go from 45 to 90 minutes.

Memberships are available if you plan to come regularly. A float membership runs $67 a month; the sauna is $38 a month. Those prices are a fair bit below the casual rates, so the maths works out quickly if Braybrook is convenient for you.

The venue has 568 Google reviews at a 4.9 rating. Reviews highlight the massage therapists by name and mention the float rooms being kept clean.

Card payments carry a small surcharge (1.10% plus $0.20), so bring cash if that bothers you. Parking at the Home and Co. complex is plentiful.
