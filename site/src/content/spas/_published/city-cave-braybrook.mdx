City Cave is an Australian chain with locations across the country and into the US, and the Braybrook outpost sits inside Home and Co., the large retail and lifestyle complex on Ballarat Rd. The format here is float therapy, infrared sauna, and massage. There's no mineral pool or cold plunge.

Float therapy is the centrepiece. Water at body temperature, a heavy load of Epsom salt, and you sit right on the surface with no effort required. Each session is private, so there's nothing to navigate socially. Whether the idea of lying still in a dark pod sounds immediately appealing or takes a moment to warm to depends on the person, but the setup is about as low-friction as it gets. Casual sessions start at $89.

The infrared sauna runs on radiant heat rather than hot air, and red light therapy is available as a bolt-on. There are several combination packages, from sauna-and-float pairings through to a three-treatment block covering all three services. Couples can add on to any package for $20.

Massage covers remedial, sports, pregnancy, and relaxation across three session lengths. If you'd rather not commit to a rolling membership, multi-session packs let you buy in blocks at reduced rates.

<Pull>The venue has 553 Google reviews at a 4.9 rating. The consistent thread in what people mention is staff who take the work seriously and sessions that run their full length. That second point is worth noting because it's not always the case in high-volume treatment venues.</Pull>

Open seven days from 8:30 am to 8:30 pm. With the layered pricing on offer, it's worth thinking roughly how often you'd come back before you book. The membership rates start to look genuinely worthwhile once you're going more than once or twice a month.
