City Cave Braybrook sits inside Home and Co., the large retail and lifestyle complex on Ballarat Rd in Braybrook's western fringe. It's one outpost of a chain that now runs across Australia and the US, and the offer here is the same across the network: float therapy, infrared sauna, and massage, with no mineral pools or cold plunge in the picture.

The float pools are the centrepiece. They're filled with water held at body temperature and a high concentration of Epsom salt — enough that you float without trying. This is the kind of thing that either sounds immediately appealing or takes a little convincing; the mechanics are simple and the format is private, so there's no social element to navigate. Casual sessions are $89; a monthly membership brings that to $67, or $59 on a weekly plan. Multi-session packs (4, 8, or 12 floats) are also available if you'd rather commit without a rolling membership.

The infrared sauna uses radiant heat rather than conventional hot air, and red light therapy is available as a bolt-on. Sauna sessions run $49 casual. If you want to combine services, there are bundled packages: Flauna (sauna + float, $129), Saunage (sauna + 45-minute massage, $159), Floatage (float + 45-minute massage, $189), and the Complete Wellness package — 45-minute sauna, 60-minute float, 45-minute massage — at $229. Couples pricing adds $20 to any of those.

Massage covers the usual range: remedial, sports, pregnancy, and relaxation, in 45-, 60-, and 90-minute blocks. A 45-minute session is $119 casual; 60 minutes is $139; 90 minutes is $199. Membership holders get reduced rates across the board.

The venue has 553 Google reviews at a 4.9 rating. The consistent theme in what people mention is staff who genuinely care and sessions that run their full length — that's worth noting because it's not always the case in high-volume treatment venues.

City Cave Braybrook opens at 8:30 am seven days a week and closes at 8:30 pm. Given the range of packaged options and membership tiers, it's worth thinking through what you want before booking — the pricing structure rewards people who know how often they'll come back.
