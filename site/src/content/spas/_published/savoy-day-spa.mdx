Underground, in the centre of Hobart, sits what the venue bills as the only communal bathhouse in Tasmania. That's a distinction worth taking seriously. If you want this kind of setup, magnesium pool, hot spas, steam room, sauna, all under one roof on a flat entry fee, Elizabeth Street is currently your only option in the state.

The water circuit covers a useful spread of temperatures, from the cooler magnesium pool up through the spas and into the heat rooms. There's no cold plunge, so the contrast side of things is limited to what the magnesium pool offers after a session in the hotter options. The building dates to an era when bathhouses were civic infrastructure rather than a niche weekend activity.

<Pull>At $40 drop-in with no booking required, it's structured more like a neighbourhood bathhouse than a day spa. Which is the point.</Pull>

The drop-in format is genuinely unusual in this space. Turn up, pay, use the facilities at your own pace. Towel hire is available if you'd rather travel light. The treatment side of the business, massages, facials, Aveda body treatments using locally sourced ingredients, runs separately from the bathhouse entry and would need its own booking.

At 231 Google reviews and a 4 out of 5 rating, the signal is solid without being unqualified. Value for money and the recently updated sauna get consistent mentions, and several visitors describe using it as a casual recovery or social session. A few reviews have flagged cleanliness as inconsistent, with some noting visible scum around the pool walls and dust in the change areas. It's worth going in with realistic expectations about the facilities rather than expecting something newly built.

<Pull>Several visitors report spending two to three hours there without any pressure to move on. The drop-in format means you can take your time.</Pull>

Hours run Monday to Friday 9am to 8pm, Saturday 9am to 6pm, and Sunday 10am to 5pm. Public holiday hours aren't fixed, so it's worth calling ahead on (03) 6224 1586 before making the trip on those days.
