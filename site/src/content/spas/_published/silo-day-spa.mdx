Silo Day Spa sits inside Peppers Silo Hotel at 91 Lindsay Street, Invermay, housed in the converted grain silos that have become one of Launceston's more recognisable addresses. It's a small, treatment-focused operation: body work and massage, handled by people who know what they're doing, with no self-guided bathing circuit to speak of.

<Pull>The spa carries a 4.7 rating across 18 Google reviews, with guests consistently pointing to individual therapists by name. That kind of specificity tends to signal quality that's personal rather than incidental.</Pull>

Treatments run to body packages and massage. Reviewers have mentioned the Solo Body Bliss package and warm oil massage in particular. The full menu lives on the website, which is worth checking before you book since it changes.

Being inside a hotel doesn't close the door to anyone. The spa takes bookings from day visitors who aren't staying at Peppers, so it works as a standalone option for anyone passing through Launceston or based elsewhere in the city.

<Pull>It's open Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00am to 6:00pm, and closed Monday and Sunday. Worth keeping in mind if you're trying to fit it around a weekend visit.</Pull>

Eighteen reviews is a modest sample, but a 4.7 rating that's held by specific, named accounts of specific treatments carries more weight than a larger pool of vague ones. The pattern here is consistent.

Bookings go through the website or by phone on (03) 6700 0650. Given the size of the operation, availability on any given day is likely limited. Calling ahead is the safer move.
