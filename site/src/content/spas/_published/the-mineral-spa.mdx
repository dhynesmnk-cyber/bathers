The Mineral Spa sits on Main Road in Hepburn Springs, in Victoria's mineral spring country. The draw here is straightforward: natural mineral bathing waters in The Retreat, backed by a sauna, steam room, and a full treatment menu if you want more than a soak.

The Retreat itself holds two spa pools and a plunge pool arranged together — the kind of setup where you can move between heat and cold at your own pace. A sauna and steam room are also part of the circuit. Water and herbal teas are provided, and session numbers are kept limited, so you're unlikely to be jostling for space.

The treatment menu covers facials, massage, body treatments, and Signature Mineral Rituals. During the winter promotion, a 90-minute spa treatment comes with a complimentary body scrub, and Royal or Purifying Rituals include Retreat passes — worth knowing if you're trying to get the most out of a single visit. The 2-for-1 Retreat Pass pricing is also worth looking up before you book if you're coming as a couple.

A staff-led orientation is part of the arrival experience, which is useful if it's your first time through the door. Robes and towels are supplied, so there's nothing bulky to pack.

The venue rates 4.1 across around 350 Google reviews, with guests consistently pointing to the limited-numbers policy and the combination of pools and sauna as highlights.

The spa is open seven days: Monday to Thursday 10am–5pm, Friday 10am–7pm, Saturday 9am–8pm, and Sunday 9am–7pm. Access is for guests 16 and over. Accommodation is available on site, and the venue also runs corporate retreat packages. Book ahead — this region draws weekend visitors from Melbourne year-round, and the good Saturday morning slots won't wait.
