The Mineral Springs Hotel opened in 1935 and the building wears that history with some confidence — formal Italian gardens, a miniature hedge maze, and an interior by designer Stuart Rattle. It sits on Main Road in Hepburn Springs, squarely in the Daylesford region's mineral water country.

The spa side — marketed as the Retreat — centres on multiple mineral spas, a cool plunge pool, dual hydrotherapy baths, saunas, and a steam room. The specific mineral content and sauna type aren't detailed in the record, though the broader context suggests dry heat. What is documented: five dual therapy rooms, a relaxation lounge, and a treatment menu covering massage, facials, body treatments, mineral rituals, and cocoons, with Pevonia Botanica and Sodashi products and packaged options.

The Retreat has an age floor of 16, so it's not suited to families with younger children. The record doesn't specify whether the spa is accessible to day visitors who aren't hotel guests.

With 528 Google reviews averaging 4.4 stars, the spa treatments and staff come up well, and the garden setting gets consistent attention. Accommodation reviews are more mixed, which is worth knowing if you're considering a stay rather than just a spa visit.
