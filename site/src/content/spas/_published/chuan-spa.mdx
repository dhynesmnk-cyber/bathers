On the ninth floor of the Langham hotel on Southgate Avenue, Chuan Spa draws on Traditional Chinese Medicine philosophy — balance of the five elements, Yin and Yang applied to water, heat and treatment sequencing. The facility itself is straightforward: an indoor pool overlooking the Southbank skyline, a jacuzzi, a sauna, a steam room, and a treatment menu covering facials, massages and packaged programs.

<Pull>The pool overlooks the Southbank skyline from level 9.</Pull>

The treatment menu extends into less conventional territory. Through a partnership with Vivve Synergi, the spa offers doctor-led, nurse-administered IV vitamin infusions — a service that marks the venue's positioning within urban wellness provision.

Standard treatment pricing sits at $175 per 60 minutes for Langham members booking on weekdays, a rate that includes sparkling and a take-home gift. A $20 surcharge applies to facials, massages and packages under other conditions. Day spa users who book a treatment receive complimentary access to the pool and thermal facilities for that day. No standalone facility-only entry is documented.

<Pull>Day spa users who book a treatment receive complimentary facility access — the pool and thermal rooms are not offered separately.</Pull>

The sauna type is not specified, which matters if you have a preference. There is no cold plunge documented. Ring (03) 8696 8111 for further detail.

The facility opens at 7am on weekdays and 8am on weekends, closing at 9pm both days. Treatment hours are shorter: weekdays run from 10am to 6pm, Saturdays from 9:30am, Sundays and public holidays from 10am. Amendments to bookings require 24 hours' notice; cancellations inside that window incur a 50% fee, and no-shows are charged in full. Gift vouchers are available.
