Out on the Adventure Way past Cunnamulla, in a stretch of outback Queensland where the horizon runs long and flat, there's a small operation doing something genuinely unusual: natural artesian water baths filled with milky grey mineral clay, each tub individual, each session under the open sky. It's not a day spa in any conventional sense — it's a paddock setup built around a geothermal water source, and the simplicity of the concept is a large part of what makes it work.

The water is warm artesian water drawn from the Great Artesian Basin, one of the largest and deepest freshwater reservoirs on earth. The clay is mineral-rich; reviewers consistently describe the experience as therapeutic. The sequence is straightforward: soak in the tub, get out and apply the mud pack over your body, let it dry beside the pot belly heater, then return to the bath to rinse off, finishing with a hot shower. That process runs to around 90 minutes depending on the session.

<Pull>Each tub is styled individually, and the whole thing takes place in what reviewers call a private, secluded area — meaningful in a region where the nearest significant town is a long way back down the road.</Pull>

Nibbles and a drink of your choice are included in the package. The host, Edyta, is mentioned by name in multiple reviews and described as friendly, knowledgeable, and genuinely attentive. At a 4.9 rating across 82 Google reviews, the feedback is consistent.

Eulo is a small outback town, and getting there requires intent — this isn't a stopover you stumble onto. If you're travelling the Adventure Way between Cunnamulla and Thargomindah, it fits naturally into the route; otherwise, it's a destination in itself. The venue is open every day from 6:30 AM to 8:30 PM, so early starts and late afternoon sessions are both on the table. Reach them on 0405 030 707 to confirm your booking before making the drive out.
