At Springs Lane, Fingal — well south of the Peninsula's main tourist drag — a working geothermal source has been given over to bathing at a scale unusual in Victoria. The water is drawn from underground and described as mineral-rich; its precise composition is not publicly detailed, but it is geothermal in the strict sense: it arrives warm from the earth rather than being heated from the mains.

The site divides into two operating precincts. The Bath House accommodates families; the Spa Dreaming Centre is reserved for adults. Both draw on the same source water. Specific temperatures across the various pools are not published in available materials, and the presence of saunas or cold plunge facilities is not documented.

Spa treatments are available across a range of styles, drawing on global therapeutic traditions. All treatments can be packaged with a bathing session, and the venue runs complimentary events included with bathing entry. The treatment offering is broad enough that a visit could occupy most of a day.

The grounds include multiple dining precincts with fresh seasonal menus, along with filtered drinking water available throughout the site. For longer stays, accommodation comes in two forms: glamping and Eco Lodge, with private ensuites and king beds. The combination of bathing, treatment, dining, and overnight accommodation means Peninsula Hot Springs functions as a destination rather than a day venue — though day visits remain standard for most.

The site operates from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM Monday to Thursday, 7:00 AM to 2:00 AM Friday, 5:00 AM to 2:00 AM Saturday, and 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM Sunday. Bookings for treatments and the Spa Dreaming Centre should be made in advance; the Bath House's arrangements are best confirmed directly. The venue is at 140 Springs Lane, Fingal, reachable on (03) 5950 8777.
