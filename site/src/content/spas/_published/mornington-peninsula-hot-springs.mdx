Peninsula Hot Springs sits on natural grounds at 140 Springs Lane, Fingal, about ninety minutes from Melbourne on the lower Mornington Peninsula, and draws geothermal mineral water straight from the earth beneath it. That's the core of what it is: a working hot springs, with the pools and the setting built around a genuine natural source.

The site splits into two distinct bathing areas. The Bath House is open to all ages. The Spa Dreaming Centre is adults-only, which is worth knowing before you book if a quieter experience is what you're after. Both draw from the same geothermal source beneath the property. Specific temperatures aren't published, though reviews mention pools running well into the forties, with one visitor singling out a 42°C pool as a highlight, and a cool room that reviewers describe as a genuine contrast worth not skipping.

Spa treatments are available alongside bathing, drawing on global traditions and intended to complement a day in the water rather than replace it. Beyond the pools, there are multiple dining precincts including the Bath House Café & Bar with seasonal menus, and accommodation in the form of glamping and Eco Lodge options for anyone who wants to make a night of it. Filtered drinking water is available throughout the site.

A couple of recurring events are worth factoring in. Sunday Sessions bring live local acoustic music from 12pm each week, included with your bathing entry. Through July and August, Bathe in Cinema adds outdoor film screenings on a weekly basis, also complimentary with bathing.

With more than 14,900 Google reviews averaging 4.5 stars, the place clearly lands for most people who make the trip. The most consistent note in detailed reviews is that weekday and daytime sessions give you more room to move between pools and let the surroundings do more of the work. Friday and Saturday nights draw bigger crowds, and if some pools happen to be closed the remaining space can feel tight.

Hours run from 7am Monday to Thursday and from 5am on weekends, with Friday and Saturday nights extending to 2am. Book ahead, particularly for weekend daytime slots. The place draws serious numbers.
