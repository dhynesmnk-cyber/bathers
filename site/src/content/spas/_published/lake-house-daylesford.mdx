Lake House sits at the edge of Lake Daylesford, at 4 King St. The lake is visible from the property, waterfall streams run through the grounds, and there's a country-town quietness to Daylesford that has been drawing people here for well over a century.

The bathing offer is a hot mineral soak, though the venue doesn't publish its mineral composition, so don't plan around magnesium if that's your reason for coming. The spa runs treatment services built around package access rather than standalone drop-ins. The Winter Cocoon Package bundles accommodation, daily breakfast with pastries from local producer Dairy Flat Farm, a farm visit, fireside amenities, and $200 per room in spa credit, from $1,000 per person twin share. There's no cold plunge, sauna, or heat circuit here. It's a hotel spa with a mineral soak, not a dedicated bathhouse circuit.

The two Chef Hat restaurant is a significant part of what Lake House offers. It overlooks the lake and runs a multi-course, fine-dining format with a serious wine list. For a lot of guests the dinner ends up being the centrepiece of the stay rather than an afterthought.

At 4.6 stars from nearly 970 Google reviews, the place rates well. People come back for three things: the spa treatments (staff are named repeatedly in reviews, which usually signals genuine attentiveness), the food and service at the restaurant, and the overall feel of the property. Cosy fireplaces, walks around the nearby lakes, and easy access to Daylesford's shops and galleries all get a mention.

If you're coming primarily for a dedicated thermal circuit, a two-hour sauna session, cold plunge, extended float, Lake House isn't the right fit. If a few nights around Lake Daylesford with good food, a mineral soak, and spa treatments folded into a single package sounds right, check lakehouse.com.au or call (03) 5348 3329 to see what's available for your dates.
