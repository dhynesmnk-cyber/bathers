Lake House sits on the edge of Lake Daylesford at 4 King St, a lakeside hotel with two Chef Hats on its restaurant and a hot mineral soak that forms the centrepiece of its spa offering. Waterfall streams run through the grounds.

The spa side is built around packages rather than a standalone bathing circuit. The mineral soak is 100% hot mineral water — the region sits on one of the largest concentrations of mineral springs in the Southern Hemisphere — though the source temperature and pool specifics aren't listed. Massage is offered through the spa packages; the full treatment menu isn't publicly detailed. The sauna type, cold plunge, and other secondary amenities aren't documented, so the mineral soak is the verified draw.

<Pull>The spa packages are structured around an overnight stay, so this is less a drop-in bathhouse and more a place where the whole visit — mineral water, dinner, a night in the lodge — is the thing.</Pull>

The Winter Cocoon Package runs from $1,000 per person twin share and includes $200 spa credit per room and a complimentary trip to Dairy Flat Farm, with their pastries served at breakfast.

The restaurant carries weight here. Rated 4.6 across nearly 970 Google reviews, guests consistently single out the multi-course dinner, the wine list, and the lakeside setting. The spa also draws mentions — one reviewer highlights the Sweet Dreams Ritual package — suggesting the treatments land well when the staffing is on.

<Pull>The consistent thread across reviews is that the whole package — water, food, setting — holds together better than any single element in isolation.</Pull>

Practically: spa access is tied to accommodation, so this isn't somewhere to book a soak on a Tuesday afternoon without also booking a room. If you're making a Daylesford trip of it, the $200 spa credit built into the Cocoon Package makes the mineral soak part of a larger stay rather than an add-on. The hotel's number is (03) 5348 3329.
