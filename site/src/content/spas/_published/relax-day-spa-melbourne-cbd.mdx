On Victoria Street in North Melbourne, a short walk from Queen Victoria Market, Relax Day Spa is a treatment-focused venue built around massage, bodywork, and a handful of complementary wellness offerings. There are no thermal pools or saunas in the core model here — what the place is actually set up for is hands-on work: Thai massage, Swedish massage, hot stone therapy, foot massage, facials, and couples treatments, with yoga, meditation, and fitness programs rounding out the offering.

The foot massage is a considered version of the thing — it opens with a hot towel warm-up, moves into Thai technique, and finishes with a dedicated spa cream. Hot stone therapy is available both as a standalone service and bolted onto a massage. Couples packages exist if you're booking for two.

The space has dedicated spa rooms, natural design elements, soft background music, and aromatic scents. Pricing isn't published in the record here, though a reviewer noted paying $292 for a couples massage package that included use of an infrared sauna, which gives some orientation for the upper end of the menu. One review called out a 90-minute couples Thai massage at medium-to-hard pressure as the best massage the writer had experienced; another described a body wrap, hot stone massage, and foot massage session positively. With 518 Google reviews at 4.5 stars, the overall signal is strong, though some reviews flag inconsistency in pressure and technique, so it's worth being specific with your therapist at the start of a session about what you're after.

The venue is open every day from 9:30am to 9:00pm, which makes it more flexible than a lot of comparable places in the city. Day spa packages are available. Booking ahead is the sensible move, particularly for couples treatments or longer packages where room availability will be the constraint.
