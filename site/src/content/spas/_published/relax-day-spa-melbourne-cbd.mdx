On Victoria Street in North Melbourne, a short walk from Queen Victoria Market, Relax Day Spa runs a dense treatment menu out of dedicated spa rooms with a nature-inspired fitout and background music. The focus here is entirely on the service rather than any kind of circuit.

There's no cold plunge and no communal pool. What the menu offers instead is range: Thai massage (traditional deep-tissue), Swedish, couples treatments, hot stone add-ons, body scrubs, wraps, facials, a Japanese head spa signature, and several foot-focused options. Packages bundle these into timed blocks from 90 minutes to four and a half hours, which is how most people seem to structure a visit.

<Pull>Two packages bring an infrared sauna into the mix, the Relax and Refresh (120 minutes, foot spa plus Swedish massage, $190) and the Relax and Detox (120 minutes, foot treatment plus body scrub, $195).</Pull>

At the longer end, the Relax and Indulge (3.5 hours, $335) and the Relax and Royale (4.5 hours, $495) both include a spa bath. The venue doesn't list temperature or mineral details for it, so if those things matter to you, a call to 0401 971 908 before booking is a sensible move.

With 518 Google reviews averaging 4.5 stars, the feedback runs positive overall. The Thai massage draws consistent praise in particular. As with most treatment-focused spas, pressure and technique can vary by practitioner, so it's worth saying what you need at the start of the session.

<Pull>Packages run from $150 for 90 minutes up to $495 for the full four-and-a-half hours, a reasonable spread depending on how much of an afternoon you want to give it.</Pull>

The spa is open daily, 9:30am to 9:00pm. Online booking works for one or two people; groups of three or more need to submit an enquiry form. A non-refundable deposit locks in the booking, cancellations require 48 hours' notice, and rescheduling needs at least three hours. Arriving five to ten minutes early is advised, since a late arrival may eat into your session time. Afterpay is accepted, but carries a surcharge of 6% or more on top of the listed price.
