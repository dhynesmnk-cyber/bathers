Crown Spa Melbourne sits on Level 3 of Crown Towers in Southbank, inside one of the country's larger hotel and entertainment complexes. The thing that sets it apart from most city day spas is the pool: one of the largest indoor pools in Australia, which is a genuinely unusual feature at this scale.

The treatment menu leans toward the higher end. La Prairie facials and Subtle Energies body treatments are the signature offerings, alongside massage. The full menu runs longer than what's publicly listed, so a call to (03) 9292 6182 is the quickest way to get the detail. There's no cold plunge here, and the model is treatment-led rather than a drop-in bathhouse setup.

<Pull>The pool is one of the largest indoors in the country, an unusual feature for a city day spa.</Pull>

Access works on hotel logic: you book a treatment, and the pool and sauna come with it. Visitors typically get an hour in those facilities before their appointment, with robes, slippers, and towels provided as standard, plus tea and snacks. Worth knowing before you go: the sauna and spa pool are gender-separated, which can be a factor if you're coming as a couple or a group hoping to use those spaces together.

<Pull>Robes, slippers, towels, tea, and snacks are included with a treatment, the kind of detail that makes a mid-afternoon booking feel like more than just the appointment itself.</Pull>

It carries a 4-star rating across 216 Google reviews, with consistent mentions of therapist attentiveness and the quality of the facilities. The Southbank address puts it close to the arts precinct and the river, so it fits naturally into a longer afternoon in that part of the city.

Open daily from 10am to 8pm. The spa sits inside Crown Towers, so a treatment booking is how you get in. Valet parking is available at the towers if you're driving.
