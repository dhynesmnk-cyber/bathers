Crown Spa sits on Level 3 of Crown Towers in Southbank — a treatment-led hotel spa built around one of the largest indoor pools in the country and a treatment menu that runs to La Prairie facials, Subtle Energies body treatments, massages, and hair and beauty services.

The setting matches what you'd expect from a five-star hotel spa: fully equipped gymnasium on site, robes and slippers provided, and locker rooms that make the transition in and out feel considered. Pool and sauna temperatures aren't documented, so if those details matter to your visit, call ahead on (03) 9292 6182.

<Pull>One of the largest indoor pools in the country sits at its centre — a genuine point of difference in a city of hotel spas.</Pull>

The spa draws 216 Google reviews to a rating of 4 out of 5, with consistent feedback on strong therapist quality, well-maintained facilities, and the sauna-and-pool access that comes with a treatment booking. One reviewer flagged the gender separation of the sauna and spa pool as a limitation for couples or groups — worth factoring in if you're planning a visit together.

La Prairie facials sit at the prestige end of Australian spa menus, so expect pricing to match. Specific costs aren't documented here — the full menu is on the venue's website.

<Pull>Treatment bookings include an hour of sauna and pool access beforehand, plus towels, robes, slippers, and complimentary teas and snacks.</Pull>

Access is through Crown Towers, open to hotel guests and external visitors alike who book a treatment. Valet parking is available. The spa runs daily, 10am to 8pm — no sitting structure, since this is a treatment spa rather than a bathing circuit, so the length of your visit depends on what you book.
