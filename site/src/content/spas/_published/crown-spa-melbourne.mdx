On the third level of Crown Towers, Crown Spa occupies a position within one of the more prominent buildings on the Southbank skyline. The spa is large in scale, hotel-grade in finish, and oriented toward treatment-room appointments rather than the circuit-style thermal bathing found elsewhere in the city.

The pool is the most notable structural element. Described as one of the largest indoor pools in the country, it anchors the leisure facilities alongside a fully equipped gymnasium — both positioned as amenities that complement the core offer of treatments.

That core offer is built around partnerships with established treatment brands. La Prairie facials sit at the more formal end of the menu; Subtle Energies body treatments bring an Ayurvedic-influenced line to the table. Massages and hair and beauty services round out a program that reads more like a full-service hotel spa than a dedicated thermal facility. No sauna, steam room, or cold plunge is documented in the available record — worth noting for visitors whose primary interest is contrast bathing rather than treatments.

The venue is open daily from 10am to 8pm. Booking ahead for treatments is advisable; pool and gymnasium access arrangements are not specified in the available record. Enquiries: (03) 9292 6182 or through the Crown Hotels website.
