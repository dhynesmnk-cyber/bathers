On Bay Street in Port Melbourne, just outside the CBD proper, Saltair is a treatment-focused day spa with no pools, no saunas, and no cold plunge. What it does have is a well-developed massage and facial menu, IPL technology, and 311 Google reviews sitting at 4.7 stars, the kind of number that reflects people coming back.

The massage side runs from relaxation and therapeutic work through to deep tissue and a signature treatment built around custom aromatherapy oil blends, developed from the original Torquay location. Pregnancy massage is available too. For facials, Saltair works with BABOR across both the luxury spa range and the more clinical DR BABOR MediSpa line, which targets specific skin concerns rather than general upkeep.

<Pull>Port Melbourne is the only Saltair location open Wednesday and Friday evenings until 7pm, which makes it a realistic option if you're coming from the city after work.</Pull>

The SuperLUX IPL service covers hair reduction, pigmentation, vascular therapy, acne, and photo rejuvenation. Worth noting: IPL and LED therapy are different technologies, and LED isn't offered here. The rest of the menu rounds out with sugar-waxing and spray tans. Couples packages are available alongside individual bookings.

Pricing isn't listed publicly, so it's worth calling ahead on (03) 9331 7774 or checking the website before you book. Street parking on Bay St is generally straightforward. The venue is closed Sundays.
