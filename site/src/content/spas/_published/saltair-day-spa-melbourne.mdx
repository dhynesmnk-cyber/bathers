On Bay Street in Port Melbourne, about ten minutes from the CBD, Saltair is a two-storey building given over entirely to treatments — massage, facials, and packaged combinations of the two. There are no pools, no saunas, no cold plunge. What's here is a focused treatment menu, a staff roster that reviews point to as a genuine strength, and parking out front.

The spa takes its name and a degree of its identity from its Torquay location, with the Port Melbourne outpost drawing on the same approach. Treatments run across two main streams: massage — including the Saltair Signature range and pregnancy massage — and facials, split between BABOR luxury and MediSpa options. BABOR is the brand's skincare of choice throughout the facial menu.

Pregnancy massage gets a particular mention in the record: one reviewer noted a therapist who "clearly had excellent knowledge for pregnancy massage" and made them feel "relaxed and confident." For a category of treatment where technique and knowledge matter more than ambience, that's worth noting.

<Pull>The booking process gets called out as easy and the staff as patient with questions beforehand — useful to know if you're planning a group or gift visit and need to talk through options.</Pull>

The venue runs Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday from 10am to 6pm; Wednesday and Friday to 7pm; and Saturday from 9am to 6pm. It's closed Sunday. Parking is available. If you're considering a spa package — the kind that strings massage and facial together — it's worth calling (03) 9331 7774 ahead to confirm what's current and lock in a specific therapist if you have one in mind.
