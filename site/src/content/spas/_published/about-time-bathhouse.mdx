On a residential street in Torquay, a short drive from the main beach strip, About Time Bathhouse runs a multi-pool bathing circuit alongside saunas, a steam room, cold plunges, and gardens set among Australian native plantings. It's not a hot-springs resort and it's not a hotel spa — it sits somewhere in between: a dedicated bathhouse with a treatment menu attached.

The grounds are one of the things reviewers keep mentioning. Fire pits, native gardens, and quiet zones are part of the setup, which gives the place more room to move than a purely indoor operation. The pools run at different temperatures, so there's a progression available if you want it — warm soak, cold plunge, repeat — though how you use the space is up to you.

The cold plunge has drawn particular attention in reviews, described as well-calibrated rather than just cold for the sake of it. The steam room also gets mentioned as a highlight. Massage and facial treatments are offered separately from general bathing access, and at least one named therapist — Katie — has been called out specifically for facials.

Sessions are timed: at least one visitor booked a 45-minute slot, and time moves faster than expected. The group booking process has been flagged as easy to navigate, which is useful if you're organising something for multiple people. Midweek sessions exist and are busy but not overwhelming.

<Pull>At least one visitor found the layout a little unclear on first arrival — no obvious guided circuit — but worth knowing going in rather than expecting a structured walkthrough.</Pull>

With a 4.7 rating across 153 Google reviews, the place is well-regarded across a range of visit types — solo, couples, and group weekends all feature in the review record.

Specific pricing isn't documented publicly, so it's worth confirming costs when you book. Hours vary by day: Monday and Wednesday open at 7am and close at 7pm; Thursday through Saturday run to 8pm; Tuesday starts at 9am; and Sunday opens at 8am and closes at 7pm. The phone number is (03) 5242 5688.
