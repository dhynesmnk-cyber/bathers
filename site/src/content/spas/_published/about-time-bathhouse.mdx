About Time Bathhouse sits on Baines Crescent in Torquay, a few minutes back from the beach, and everything here is built around magnesium water: a large indoor pool, outdoor social pools, and hot pools across a range of temperatures. The cold plunges drop to 8°C–11°C, which is cold enough for the contrast to register properly.

The heat circuit is well-stocked for a regional venue. There are two saunas at meaningfully different temperatures, a steam room running at the milder end, and a separate room combining chromotherapy, steam, and salt therapy. Four distinct heat environments in total, which shapes how you'd want to pace a 90-minute session.

Outside, fire pits sit among Australian native plantings. There's space to pause between rounds rather than cycle straight from one heat room to the next, which is useful when you're not sure how your body is tracking. Massage services are available on-site, though the current pricing and session lengths aren't listed publicly, the front desk would have those details.

The venue holds a 4.7 rating across 153 Google reviews. Plenty of those single out the cold plunge and the saunas, and a few mention that the 45-minute session moves faster than it sounds on paper, which is worth factoring in when you're deciding which format to book.

Sessions start at $49 for 45 minutes or $69 for 90, with different rates on weekends and public holidays. The shorter format is only available Monday to Thursday, subject to availability. Regular visitors will find the multi-pass packs worthwhile: 10 sessions for $520 or 25 for $1,150, both covering 90-minute social bathing. Towel hire is $5 if you'd rather not carry one. Minimum age is 16. Weekend slots fill up, so booking ahead is worth doing.
