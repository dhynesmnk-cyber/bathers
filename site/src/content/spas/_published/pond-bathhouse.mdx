Pond Bathhouse sits on Ross Street in Mornington, founded by Dr. Sally Shaw — a medical background that shows in the venue's emphasis on contrast therapy as deliberate practice rather than an afterthought. The circuit runs magnesium mineral pools, infrared saunas, traditional steam rooms, and ice baths, with private meditation and relaxation spaces built into the sequence.

The standout detail: a hard cap of 20 patrons at any one time. For anyone who's queued for a cold plunge at a packed urban bathhouse, that number carries real weight.

Treatments run alongside the bathing circuit rather than separately — massage therapy, a salt scrub and shower ritual, and contrast therapy are all on offer. The changerooms are well-appointed: Dyson hair dryers, hair ties, quality shower products, and refreshments (teas, hydralytes, infused water, nuts) available throughout your session. The relaxation and meditation beds come with blankets and heaters.

Google reviews sit at 4.8 stars across 281 ratings, with consistent flags on massage quality, staff attentiveness, and the uncrowded atmosphere.

Two hours felt like the right amount of time according to one regular — useful intelligence before you go.

Pricing isn't publicly listed, so confirm directly before you book. Hours vary day to day; the earliest start is 7:30am on Wednesdays and Saturdays, and the latest close is 9pm on Mondays, Thursdays, and Fridays. Given the cap on numbers, booking ahead makes sense, especially for weekend mornings and the longer Friday evening window.
