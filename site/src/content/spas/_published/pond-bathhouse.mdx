Pond Bathhouse on Ross Street in Mornington runs a hard cap of 20 guests at any one time. That single policy shapes what the place is like in practice, and it's the thing reviewers keep coming back to. Across 281 Google ratings, the place sits at 4.8 out of 5, and "no overcrowding" is one of the phrases that comes up again and again.

The water circuit pairs magnesium mineral pools with an ice bath, while heat comes from both infrared saunas and a traditional steam room. That distinction matters: two genuinely different heat experiences gives you real variety in how you move through a session. Private meditation spaces and heated relaxation lounges round out the floor, so there's somewhere to properly stop between rounds rather than perching on a bench.

The venue was founded by Dr. Sally Shaw, a medical doctor, which may explain the level of operational detail that reviewers keep noting: sauna towels restocked throughout a session, hydralytes and infused water on hand, Dyson dryers in the changerooms. These feel less like perks and more like someone thinking carefully about what a two-hour visit should actually involve.

Treatments run alongside the bathing circuit. The Total Reset Package ($195 for 120 minutes) combines 60 minutes in the bathhouse with a 60-minute massage, which is the most practical option if you're driving out from elsewhere and want to make an afternoon of it. A salt scrub and shower are also available. The venue doesn't list a bathhouse-only price on the website, so it's worth a call to 0455 335 633 if that's what you're after.

Hours shift across the week. Wednesday and Saturday open at 7:30am, Thursday starts at 10am, and both weekend days close at 6pm rather than 8 or 9pm. Worth checking before you plan a late session. With a 20-person cap and a venue that rates this consistently, booking ahead through the website is the sensible move, walking in on a Friday afternoon and finding room is unlikely.
