Underneath Constitution Hill Road, Aurora runs a subterranean circuit built around magnesium-enriched water and the alternating logic of hot and cold. It's a proper bathhouse in the European sense, with a structured 11-step journey rather than a loose collection of pools and rooms. The sequence moves you through thermal lounges, sauna, steam room, salt room, sensory showers, cold plunge, and a glacial ice room, with hydration stations placed along the way.

The magnesium pools are the centrepiece. Magnesium-rich water is understood to support circulation and lymphatic drainage, and shifting between warm and cold water activates the parasympathetic nervous system, the calming response. The glacial ice room gives you a sharper cold option alongside the plunge pool, so there's more than one way to run the contrast sequence depending on how much cold you want at once.

<Pull>The salt room replicates the microclimate of a salt cave, which puts it in a different category from standard steam or sauna space.</Pull>

The treatment menu sits alongside the circuit as an optional layer. At 4.8 stars across 613 Google reviews, the bathhouse draws comparisons to European equivalents. The treatment staff are specifically noted by reviewers for attentiveness and technique. Entry covers towel, locker, shower access, hair dryers, and swimwear water extractors, so there's little to bring beyond your bathers.

If you're likely to return, the maths on session packs is straightforward: five sessions saves $110 against single-session pricing, and ten sessions saves $275. Both are valid for a year from the first use. The minimum age for bathhouse access is 14. Hours run Monday to Thursday 9am to 7pm, Friday to 8pm, Saturday from 8am to 8pm, and Sunday 8am to 6pm.
