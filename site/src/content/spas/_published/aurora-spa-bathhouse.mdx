On Constitution Hill Road in Sorrento, behind the limestone ridgeline that divides Port Phillip Bay from the ocean, Aurora has built its bathhouse underground. The structure is all-weather and subterranean — deliberate insulation from the Peninsula's variable coastal climate — and it houses an 11-step ritual circuit that moves visitors through thermal pools, heat, and chill in a fixed sequence.

The thermal pools are the centre of the operation, though their temperature and mineral composition are not publicly stated. Heat facilities exist, but whether that means a Finnish-style sauna, a steam room, or something else hasn't been specified. The cold side of the circuit is similarly undocumented: chill facilities are listed, temperatures are not. What's clear is the sequencing: eleven steps, designed to be worked through rather than dipped into at random.

Treatments sit alongside the bathhouse rather than replacing it. Massage is available, as are active skincare treatments, and both draw on ASPAR — a product line that began as hand-blended formulations Aurora made for its own guests before being developed into a standalone brand. That origin story is one of the more grounded things about the venue's identity: the products came from the place, not the other way around.

For longer stays, the venue runs half-day and full-day Aurora Retreats, and corporate groups are catered for by arrangement. The retreat formats presumably include bathhouse access alongside treatments, though the exact inclusions aren't documented in detail.

Booking ahead would be prudent given the format — a structured ritual circuit doesn't lend itself to walk-ins, and the retreat packages suggest demand is managed. The venue opens at 9 AM on weekdays (8 AM on weekends) and runs until between 6 and 8 PM depending on the day, with the longest trading hours on Friday and Saturday evenings. Enquiries can be directed to (03) 5935 1222.
