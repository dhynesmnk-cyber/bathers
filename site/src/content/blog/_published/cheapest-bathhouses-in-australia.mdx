Bathing well doesn't have to cost much. Plenty of venues in the directory sell a plain adult drop-in: one price, one visit, no package and no treatment attached. Those are the ones ranked here, cheapest first, straight from the prices each venue publishes.

<ComparisonTable queryKey="cheapest" />

The lowest of them is <Superlative queryKey="cheapest" />. A cheap drop-in usually points to one of two things. Either it's a simple, well-run sauna where you pay for the heat and little else, or it's a natural spring where the water does the work and the site is kept plain on purpose. Neither is a lesser session. Some of the best ones come from the least adorned rooms.

<Pull>The price on the door is the honest number. It's the one figure a venue can't dress up, and the one worth comparing.</Pull>

A drop-in is also the fairest thing to line up side by side. Package rates fold in towels, robes, a treatment or a meal, and every venue bundles those differently, so the totals stop meaning the same thing. A single adult entry is like for like. Where a venue only sells packages or treatment-led sessions, it has no plain drop-in to rank, so it stays off this table; there's nothing to compare.

Prices move. A venue lifts its weekday rate, a new sauna opens under it, and the order here shifts. Because this page reads the prices live from the directory, the ranking above is always the current one. It updates itself as venues change what they charge.

If you're chasing the lowest cost, start at the top and work down. To sort by feature or setting instead, the [compare](/compare/) hub does that, and the [methodology](/methodology/) page explains how these prices are checked and dated.
