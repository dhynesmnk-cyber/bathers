Contrast therapy is simple in principle: heat your body thoroughly in a sauna, then immerse in cold water, and repeat. The physiological loop, blood vessels dilating, then constricting, then dilating again, is what most people are chasing. But the venues that offer it vary quite a bit in what surrounds that core circuit, and that's worth thinking through before you book.

## What kind of venue is it?

Some of the places in this table are purpose-built bathhouses where contrast bathing is the whole point. Others are hotel spas or gym facilities where a sauna and cold plunge sit alongside a broader offering. That distinction matters mainly for atmosphere and for how much time you're expected to spend there. A dedicated bathhouse tends to build its pricing and session lengths around repeated cycles; a hotel spa might treat the circuit as a warm-up to a treatment.

## Sauna type and cold plunge depth

A handful of venues here run both a traditional (Finnish-style) sauna and an infrared sauna, which means you can experiment with different kinds of heat. Traditional saunas run hotter and work faster; infrared runs at lower temperatures and suits people who find the conventional heat hard to tolerate. If you have a preference, check the amenities column before you go.

On the cold side, the records that publish plunge temperatures show real variation. My Recovery Lounge, for instance, offers a graduated option for beginners alongside a much more aggressive setting for experienced bathers, a sensible arrangement that not every venue bothers with.

## Age limits and booking requirements

A few venues set a published minimum age, so check the table if you're planning to bring a teenager. Nova Holistic Spa and Waldheim Alpine Spa both require bookings in advance; the others in this set don't, though that can change and it's always worth confirming.

## On pricing

Several venues haven't published a drop-in price, which makes direct comparison harder. Where pricing is visible, the range is meaningful. Memberships and multi-session packs appear across much of the set and tend to offer better value if you plan to go regularly. Soak Bathhouse in South Yarra has a tiered structure where time of day and day of week affect the rate, so an early weekday visit costs noticeably less than a weekend evening.

## A note on coverage

The directory currently skews towards Victoria. Venues in SA, WA, ACT and Tasmania are represented, but the Victorian options are more numerous for now. That's a reflection of where the data is, not where contrast therapy is available.
