## Nowhere else to be

A sauna is very good at one thing besides heat: it stops you doing anything else. You cannot answer email in a steam room. You cannot half-watch something while you plunge into cold water. The environment simply will not allow it. Your attention narrows to breath, skin, and temperature, and for once there is nowhere else to be.

We think that is most of the value, and it is worth naming.

## Presence without the sermon

Plenty of people will sell you presence as a technique to be practised, tracked, and optimised. Bathing does not ask for any of that. You do not need an app, a course, or the right vocabulary. The heat brings you into your body whether you have read the books or not. The cold does it faster.

Being present, here, is not an achievement. It is just what is left when the distractions are physically removed.

## A small reset

You come out of a good soak slightly rearranged. Slower, quieter, more here. The problems you brought in are still there, but you are meeting them from a steadier place. That is a modest thing to offer, and we think modest things, reliably delivered, are underrated.

Sit in the heat. Feel the cold. Do one thing. It turns out to be enough.
